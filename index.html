Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa and the most populous nation on the continent. It is bordered by Benin, Niger, Chad, and Cameroon, with a long coastline on the Gulf of Guinea. Abuja is the capital, and Lagos is the largest city and a major center of business, music, and film.

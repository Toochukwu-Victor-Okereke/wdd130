Half-Day Splash

Perfect for first-timers and families. Enjoy about three hours of gentle Class II rapids, with plenty of time to swim and soak in the scenery. Our guides teach paddling basics before you launch.

Full-Day Canyon Run

Paddle through towering canyon walls and challenging Class III and IV rapids. A hearty riverside lunch keeps energy high for the big drops in the afternoon.

Sunset Float

Relax on a calm evening float as the sky turns gold. This easy trip is ideal for couples, groups, and anyone who wants the river without the adrenaline.
